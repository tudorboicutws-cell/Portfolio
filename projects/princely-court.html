The Moldavian Princely Court of Iași

Five evolutionary stages of the Princely Court of Iași, rebuilt in 3D from historical research and printed as physical models for an exhibition at the Palace of Culture.

Role
3D Artist
Client
Iasi.Travel Association
Time
1,500+ hours (team)
Shown at
Palace of Culture, Iași
Made with

One of my biggest projects. I was part of the Iasi.Travel Association team that recreated five evolutionary stages of the Princely Court of Iași, from 1770 to 1925, so visitors can see how the court grew over the centuries.

The 3D models were made by Tiberiu Teodor Stanciu and me, then printed and shown as a physical exhibition at the Palace of Culture in Iași.
