The Moldavian Princely Court of Iași

Five centuries of a royal court, rebuilt in 3D from historical research and printed as physical models for heritage display.

Role
3D Artist / Architect (volunteer)
Client
Iasi.Travel Association
Tools
Blender, SketchUp, 3D print prep
Year
2022 – 2025

Overview

The Princely Court of Iași changed shape many times over the centuries. Working from archival plans and historical research, I modelled five evolutionary stages of the court so visitors can see how it grew.

Each stage was then optimized and split into parts for multi-stage 3D printing, turning the digital models into physical pieces for heritage preservation and public display.
