Overview

A collection of props and environment assets made for mobile and PC games. Many started as 2D concept sketches from the production team, which I turned into optimized 3D models.

The focus is on what matters in real-time: efficient polygon counts, clean UV unwrapping and PBR materials that hold up under engine lighting.
