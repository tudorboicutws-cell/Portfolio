Samdam: Web & Email Campaigns

Campaign visuals for a promotional products distributor: brand launches, seasonal offers and a loyalty program, in Romanian and Dutch.

Role
Graphic Designer
Client
Samdam Romania
Year
2023 – 2024
Made with
For

Samdam sells branded products from names like Samsonite, Hedgren, BergHOFF, Fritel, Stanley/Stella and Prodir to companies. I designed the banners for their website, newsletters and social media, in Romanian and Dutch.

The work covers product launches, seasonal campaigns (Valentine's Day, Back to School, Christmas, New Year, EURO 2024) and the Smileys loyalty program, all inside the yellow and black Samdam identity.
