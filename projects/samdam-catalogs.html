Samdam: Brand Catalogs & Newsletters

Product pages, brand stories and newsletters that present each collection clearly, from a backpack line to a full cookware range.

Role
Graphic Designer
Client
Samdam Romania
Brands
Hedgren, BergHOFF, Fritel, Samdam
Tools
Photoshop, Illustrator

Overview

Product design work for Samdam: catalog spreads for Hedgren bags and backpacks, BergHOFF cookware collections (Leo, Ron, Gem, Essentials), Fritel appliances and the Samdam brand values.

Each layout had to explain the product fast: hero photo, key features, color options and the story of the brand, ready for both print and email.
