Cultural Tourism in the Digital Age

Discovering the Royal Court of Iași through virtual reality: five historical stories turned into explorable 3D scenes.

Role
3D Artist (team project)
Client
Iasi.Travel Association
For
Palace of Culture, Iași
Made with

A project I did with my team at the Iasi.Travel Association for the Palace of Culture in Iași. The goal was to find a new way to tell the city's history and make tourism in Iași more fun.

We built five stories, each one a moment from the life of the Royal Court.
